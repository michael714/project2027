The Offer

Voters have given Democrats both chambers of Congress, and the President holds the veto. Democrats want lower costs and a stronger, more secure country for American families, and President Trump wants his place in history.

Project 2027 puts both on the table. Each prize is paired with a specific bill, each pairing is negotiated separately, and everything happens in public. Nothing is delivered until the legislation is signed.

Negotiating posture. Democrats open with the Tier 1 honors and the naming credit only, using the companion offer sheet prepared for the White House. Everything else in this memo is held in reserve and released one item at a time, as each bill advances.

The Seven Priorities

  1. Honor the Trump Presidency
  2. Lower the cost of groceries and gas
  3. Make healthcare affordable
  4. Open the door to housing for young people
  5. Clean up money and corruption in politics
  6. Rebalance the powers of the presidency
  7. Reduce dependency on the Strait of Hormuz

1Priority 1: Honor the Trump Presidency

Democrats offer President Trump recognition that only Congress can give, in exchange for his signature on the legislative priorities outlined below. Every honor is tied to the passage of a legislative act intended to improve the lives of all Americans. The honors come in three tiers, from small gestures to the largest prizes.

Tier 1 is offered up front

Congress creates the United States Presidential Peace Prize, and President Trump receives the first one. Congress also passes formal commendations for specific, documented accomplishments.

Tier 2 is negotiable

Congress authorizes the White House ballroom and underground security complex, with disclosed funding and a cost cap. It authorizes the triumphal Arch, with a fixed site, budget and design review. It also protects the redesigned Oval Office from alteration by later administrations.

Tier 3 is reserved for the biggest deals

Congress adds his name to the Kennedy Center, which a federal judge has ruled only Congress can do.1 Congress also considers his portrait on American money, which requires changing an 1866 law allowing only portraits of deceased people on U.S. currency.2 Each currency option is negotiated on its own, and each is paired with a specific bill:

  • The gold coin. Congress settles the coin’s legal status, in exchange for the grocery and gas relief package.
  • The $1 bill. Congress lifts the ban for this note, in exchange for the first-time and young homebuyer package.
  • The $250 commemorative note. Congress authorizes a new denomination bearing his portrait, in exchange for a cap on prescription drug prices and out-of-pocket costs.
  • The $100 bill. Congress lifts the ban for the nation’s top working bill, in exchange for a landmark healthcare affordability law. This is the biggest prize, and it is priced accordingly.

2Priority 2: Lower the Cost of Groceries and Gas

Families feel the price of food and fuel every week. Democrats pass a relief package aimed at both, [specific mechanisms to be added]. It is the first and smallest deal, and it opens negotiations by showing that the process works.

3Priority 3: Make Healthcare Affordable

Healthcare is the largest bill on the table. Democrats cap prescription drug prices and out-of-pocket costs, then follow with a comprehensive affordability law. These bills carry the $250 note and the $100 bill, because the biggest benefit to families earns the biggest honor.

4Priority 4: Open the Door to Housing for Young People

Buying a first home has become out of reach for a generation. Democrats pass a first-time and young homebuyer package, [specific mechanisms to be added]. It is paired with the $1 bill.

5Priority 5: Clean Up Money and Corruption in Politics

Democrats strengthen the rules on money in politics and protect the independence of the Department of Justice. These reforms are not traded for any prize, and neither is oversight. Investigations proceed on their own track, separate from the deal.

6Priority 6: Rebalance the Powers of the Presidency

Democrats pass clear limits on executive power that take effect at the start of the next term, so the rules apply to every future president equally. Democrats consider this the most valuable item in the plan. [Decision: what, if anything, is offered in exchange.]

7Priority 7: Reduce Dependency on the Strait of Hormuz

Any legislation that revises a provision of the One Big Beautiful Bill is passed as the Donald J. Trump Recognition and Betterizing America Act. The energy section of that act is the Reduce Dependency on Strait of Hormuz Act. It restores tax credits for solar panels and home batteries, restores tax credits for electric vehicles, and funds upgrades to the resilience of the power grid.

Its purpose is to protect American families and the American economy from price shocks caused by conflict overseas. Every barrel of oil the country does not need is a barrel that no foreign navy can block. The naming credit is the only recognition offered for this priority.

How the Deal Works

Five rules govern every item.

  • Nothing takes effect until the law it was traded for takes effect.
  • The biggest prizes are tied to the biggest bills.
  • Every concession is written into a bill, passed by Congress and placed on the public record.
  • There are no private gifts and no side deals.
  • Every bill in the plan carries the name Donald J. Trump, so the credit for each accomplishment is unmistakable.

The First 100 Days

Congress convenes on January 3 and introduces all seven priorities together. Democrats open talks with the White House on a first package and publish a public scorecard so voters can follow the progress.
